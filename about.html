About AMANI

Built for anglers and fly shops.

A Passion Born in the Water. A Craft Made by Hand. At Amiani's Fly Wholesalers, we believe every fly has a purpose, every detail matters, and every angler deserves confidence in what they tie to the end of their line. Based in Nairobi, Kenya, our story is built on a passion for fly fishing, dedication to craftsmanship, and a commitment to supplying quality fishing flies to the world. For over two decades, we have been perfecting the art of fly tying - bringing together skilled hands, carefully selected materials, and an understanding of the patterns that anglers trust. From delicate dry flies and technical nymphs to streamers, salmon flies, saltwater patterns, and custom designs, we produce flies for fly shops, guides, outfitters, and anglers who value quality and consistency. We are more than a wholesale fly supplier. We are a team of craftsmen who understand that behind every fly is a moment on the water, a fish pursued, and a memory waiting to be made. Handcrafted in Kenya. Trusted by anglers worldwide. Our promise is simple: to deliver flies made with care, respect the craft, and build lasting relationships with the people who share our passion.

Our fly categories can include dry flies, nymphs, streamers, terrestrials, attractors, caddis, mayflies, stoneflies, midges and many specialist patterns.
